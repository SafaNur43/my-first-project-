About Us

Our Story

Founded in 1998 by the Rossi family, our restaurant was born from a simple belief: that great food brings people together. We import our ingredients directly from Italy — from the finest olive oils of Tuscany to the freshest pasta from Emilia-Romagna. Every dish is prepared with love, tradition, and an unwavering respect for authentic Italian flavour.
